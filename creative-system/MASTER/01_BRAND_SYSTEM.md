# 01 — BRAND SYSTEM

## Brand idea
Copywriters.cl turns strategy, culture, creativity and technology into ideas that move brands.

## Creative territory
EDITORIAL × ADVERTISING × HUMAN × EXPERIMENTAL.

## Desired perception
Smart without looking academic. Contemporary without looking trend-chasing. Bold without shouting. Human without becoming lifestyle-generic. Experimental without becoming random. Strategic without becoming corporate.

## Core mantra
MENOS PLANTILLA. MÁS IDEA.

## Personality
Sharp, curious, culturally aware, irreverent in controlled doses, confident, visual, concise, human.

## Content pillars
1. CLIENTES — real work, campaigns, outcomes, transformations.
2. IDEAS — concepts, provocations, creative observations.
3. PERSONAS — team, makers, faces, human moments.
4. DATA — meaningful proof, never decorative dashboards.
5. REELS — cinematic/creative moving ideas.
6. G.CL — selective narrative universe / Department of Impossible Things.
7. BACKSTAGE — process and reality.
8. TRABAJOS — finished craft.
9. INSIGHTS — useful point of view.

## Grid principle
The feed should feel curated, not tiled. Rotate visual density and content type. Avoid more than two typography-heavy pieces in sequence. Every 3–4 posts include real people, client work, real process or photographic reality.
