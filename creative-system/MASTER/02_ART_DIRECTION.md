# 02 — ART DIRECTION

## Visual world
Think independent magazine + fashion/editorial photography + smart outdoor advertising + contemporary creative studio.

## Desired image characteristics
- cinematic but believable;
- tactile materials: paper, plastic, metal, glass, fabric, ink, tape;
- directional light and real shadows;
- deliberate imperfection/grain where useful;
- unexpected objects or situations;
- strong single focal point;
- negative space designed for type when needed;
- people should feel observed, not stock-photo posed;
- compositions can be strange, witty or slightly surreal, but remain legible.

## Composition
Use asymmetry, cropping, scale contrast, oversized type, editorial margins, tension and purposeful whitespace. Do not center everything. Do not fill every empty area.

## Pink behavior
Signal pink is an intervention: underline, tape, marker, object, single field, highlight, annotation, one word. Avoid making every background pink.

## Photography vs graphics
Prefer a strong photograph/conceptual image with restrained graphic intervention. Pure typography is allowed when the line itself is strong enough to be the idea.

## Creative bar
A post should ideally have one visual device that can be described in one sentence: “A newspaper whose headline becomes the ad,” “a laptop abandoned on a metal chair with a brutally simple claim,” etc.
