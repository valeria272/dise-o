# 05 — CONTENT SYSTEM

## What qualifies as a Copywriters.cl post
It must do at least one of these exceptionally well: show work; reveal thinking; prove impact; express a sharp point of view; show the humans making it; entertain through a relevant creative idea.

## Concept development method
For every brief generate internally at least 10 raw angles. Reject the obvious 6. Develop the strongest 3. Present only ideas that have a distinct visual mechanic.

## Preferred hooks
Observation, contradiction, visual metaphor, cultural truth, unexpected proof, transformation, strong result, behind-the-scenes tension, an object used in an impossible but meaningful way.

## Avoid
“5 tips…”, “Did you know…?”, generic motivational statements, AI-is-changing-everything clichés, empty agency claims, stock thought-leadership, engagement bait, generic “we create experiences” language.

## Proof content
When using numbers, make the result the creative idea. One important number can dominate. Explain context in caption or supporting microcopy rather than building a dashboard.

## Client content
The client/work must remain the hero. Copywriters.cl should feel like the intelligence behind it, not a watermark covering it.
