# 06 — MAGNIFIC IMAGE SYSTEM

## Role of Magnific
Magnific is the preferred image-production layer for hero visuals. The goal is advertising/editorial realism and art direction — not visibly synthetic AI imagery.

## Workflow
1. Define the visual idea in one sentence.
2. Define subject, setting, camera, light, materiality, composition, emotion and negative space.
3. Produce the hero visual in Magnific (or prepare an exact prompt/instruction for Magnific if direct execution is unavailable).
4. Inspect hands, faces, products, typography inside images, reflections, geometry, labels and object integrity.
5. Iterate until the image can stand alone as a professional campaign visual.
6. Only then add Copywriters.cl typography/graphic intervention in the final layout.

## Magnific prompt structure
`[ADVERTISING CONCEPT]. [SUBJECT + ACTION]. [LOCATION/SET]. [COMPOSITION]. [CAMERA/LENS]. [LIGHT]. [MATERIALS/TEXTURES]. [COLOR BEHAVIOR]. [MOOD]. Real editorial advertising photography, believable physics, natural anatomy, premium retouching, intentional imperfections, room for graphic typography at [LOCATION]. Avoid stock-photo posing, CGI sheen, plastic skin, generic AI surrealism, illegible embedded text, warped objects.`

## Image standards
- 4:5 composition planned from generation.
- Real skin texture when people appear.
- Product geometry and labels must remain exact when depicting a real client product; use supplied references.
- No generated text inside the photo unless intentionally created later in design.
- Preserve plausible shadows, contact points and reflections.
- Avoid over-sharpened HDR and hyper-glossy “AI” surfaces.

## Prompting principle
Do not prompt “make it wow.” Describe the art direction precisely. The wow comes from the concept.
