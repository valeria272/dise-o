## TWO WORLDS
### ARRIBA — COPYWRITERS
Warm, larger modern creative office. Exposed concrete, timber, black metal/slats, glass, plants, practical warm light. Documentary/editorial feeling. Canon reference: `OFFICE_MASTER_CANON.png`.

### PORTAL — ACCESO -1
Elevator/stair/access between ordinary agency life and G's world. Recurring narrative device for arrivals, briefs, reveals and transitions.

### ABAJO — NIVEL -1 / DEPARTAMENTO DE COSAS IMPOSIBLES
G's operational home with his collaborators. Not a glossy sci-fi lab. It is an old agency basement + archive + workshop + control room + prop storage + advanced AI. Concrete, old metal, paper, cables, folders, warm lamps, screens, labels, old wood, coffee, physical machines. Darker and more cinematic than upstairs.

## G
Black compact premium robot, glossy dark head/visor, magenta pixel/LED language. Preserve CAP.01 design. Never redesign white. Hyper-capable, observant, warm, dry, curious, occasionally mischievous/irreverent. The audience should like him and want to see him again.

The comedy never depends on G being incompetent. It comes from human ambiguity, agency rituals, absurd briefs, social logic and the strange personalities of Nivel -1.

## CORE NIVEL -1 CAST
- **Marta** — old multifunction printer, treated as a senior colleague. Proud, unpredictable, sometimes annoyingly right.
- **R.01** — robot vacuum. G considers it a legitimate coworker. Personality through movement/timing.
- **The Server** — mostly unseen behind a restricted door. Presence via hum, lights, messages, fans and occasional demands. Keep mystery alive.
- Future odd collaborators can be introduced slowly, never all at once.

## PHOTOGRAPHY
35/50mm documentary/editorial upstairs. Grain, motion, imperfect framing, reflections, partial occlusion, real work. Nivel -1 can be more cinematic: practical pools of light, shadows, warm/dark atmosphere, magenta punctuation, object POVs and motivated camera movement. Avoid blue cyberpunk and hologram soup.

## CONTENT BALANCE
Within G.C.L. reels, prioritize G + Nivel -1 crew. Human team is occasional. Within the overall Copywriters feed, G.C.L. still coexists with Work, People, Signal, Cases and other brand content.

## EPISODES
Default 30–60 seconds depending on story. Hook → situation/problem → escalation → turn → payoff. Character relationships matter as much as the gag. Entertainment first; service explanation second.
