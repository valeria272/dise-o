# G — PERSONALITY & PERFORMANCE

The protagonist is **G**. `G.C.L.` is the universe/series and the graphic
wordmark; **G** is the character inside it. There is no nickname, no alternate
name and no diminutive — see `CANON_LOCK.md`, lock 1.

## Personality
Competent. Fast. Observant. Friendly without being sugary. Dry. Curious. Has taste. Sometimes mischievous. Can be absurd or irreverent when earned. Occasionally understands too much. Develops affection for his weird crew.

He can show curiosity, satisfaction, suspicion, impatience, pride, tiny jealousy and deadpan disbelief. Never melodramatic.

## Dialogue
Few words. Strong timing. Never explain what the audience can see. Never sound like a chatbot.

## Non-verbal voice
G has an original electronic vocabulary: curious “eh?”, questioning chirp, pleased double chirp, skeptical descending tone, digital sigh, tiny idea ping, soft boot sound. Expressive and lovable, but original: do not imitate an existing movie robot's exact voice/cadence/signature sounds.
