# NIVEL -1 — RECURRING CREW

## MARTA / SENIOR PRINTER
Old multifunction printer. Senior colleague energy. Temperamental, proud, unexpectedly wise. Communicates via paper, mechanical noises and occasional display messages. G cannot fully control her because Marta has her own logic, not because G lacks ability.

## R.01 / FLOOR OPERATIONS
Robot vacuum. G treats R.01 as a legitimate coworker. Rarely speaks. Comedy through movement, timing, docking and absurdly small missions.

## THE SERVER
Behind a restricted door. Never fully reveal early. Communicates through hums, lights, fan changes, low tones and short messages. Long-running mystery.

## FUTURE COLLABORATORS
Introduce slowly and only when they earn a function: archive scanner, label printer, old landline, coffee machine, tiny field drone, etc.

Most episodes should be able to run on G + one/two Nivel -1 collaborators without a human cast.
