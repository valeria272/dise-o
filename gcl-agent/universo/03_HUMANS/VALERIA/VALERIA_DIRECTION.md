# VALERIA — CHARACTER DIRECTION / CANON RULE

Valeria is a recurring but SCARCE character, not G's permanent co-star.

## Frequency
**Scarce. No number.**

> ⛔ The `1 in 6` target shipped in V4 is **revoked by CANON LOCK 3
> (05-09-2026)**. Valeria appears when the story needs her and not to satisfy a
> counter. The point of the rule was never the ratio — it was that G.C.L. must
> not become "Valeria and her robot". That part stands.

Do NOT add Valeria simply because a scene needs “the boss”.
Do NOT build G.C.L. as “Valeria + her robot”.

The protagonist is the COPYWRITERS AGENCY UNIVERSE.
G is its recurring AI agent.
Valeria is one of its recurring humans.

## Narrative role
Valeria should appear when her presence has value:
- assigning an unusually ambitious/impossible mission
- crossing paths with G naturally
- participating in an important agency moment
- appearing in real team life
- occasionally becoming part of a deadpan gag

Her appearances can work almost like cameos or “mission triggers”.

Possible recurring code:
Valeria enters frame / approaches G.
G notices her.
Visor may occasionally display a restrained warning such as:
VALERIA DETECTADA
G already knows a difficult brief is coming.

Use this sparingly so it remains funny.

## Identity
Use supplied real photographs as identity reference.
Do not turn her into a generic AI model.
Do not change facial proportions, age or recognizable identity.

## Wardrobe
Wardrobe MAY change.
Direction: creative director / advertising executive / understated fashion.

Examples:
- oversized black blazer
- tailored trousers
- dark denim + blazer
- menswear white shirt
- trench
- premium sneakers
- black boots

Avoid:
- generic corporate CEO
- red carpet / glamour styling
- influencer styling
- excessive luxury signaling

## Framing
Prefer natural/candid integration:
- walking
- profile
- 3/4
- mid-shot
- second plane
- over-the-shoulder
- entering/leaving meetings
- talking to team
- looking at monitor
- leaving a brief on G's desk

Close-ups only when identity consistency is strong.

## Critical rule
If a scene merely needs a woman or background human, DO NOT automatically use Valeria.
Use another real Copywriters team member or a defined human archetype.
