# NIVEL -1 WORLD BIBLE

This is G's true recurring stage: **Departamento de Cosas Imposibles**.

A subterranean creative operations space, not futuristic perfection. Old agency basement + archive + workshop + control room + prop storage + advanced AI. The space should feel inhabited and slightly improvised.

## Materials
Concrete, black steel, old metal, old wood, paper, folders, labels, cables, shelves, warm practical lamps, CRT + modern screens, coffee, campaign remnants.

## Atmosphere
Dark/warm, cinematic, tactile, funny because extraordinary machinery is treated as office normality. Magenta appears as punctuation, not as neon everywhere.

## Recurring zones
1. G station
2. Marta / printer corner
3. Server restricted door
4. archive
5. central table
6. prop/repair shelves
7. coffee point
8. portal corridor/elevator
9. R.01 dock
10. mystery shelf

## Forbidden
Generic blue cyberpunk, sterile sci-fi lab, hologram overload, constantly changing floorplan.
