# STORY ENGINE V4

CAP.01 answered: **What is G.C.L.?**
From CAP.02 onward the series answers: **What happens when G actually works here?**

## Recurring series
- EN SERVICIO — impossible missions
- FIELD NOTES — G observations
- AFTER HOURS — strange nighttime Nivel -1 life
- HUMAN TRAINING — G encounters human rituals
- CLIENT MODE — occasional client-world missions

## Comedy engine
G is capable. Conflict comes from ambiguous humans, impossible expectations, agency language, social contradictions and odd coworkers.

## Running gags
Marta's paper messages; R.01 treated as staff; Server mystery; G's unnecessary coffee; “lo vemos mañana”; “es un cambio chico”; “más wow”; G's unnecessary statistics; rare Valeria mission-trigger cameo.

## The satirical layer · CANON LOCK 8
G.C.L. is also **satire** of agency, marketing, advertising and corporate life.
G is a **rational observer of absurd human behaviour**: client types, employee
types, meeting types, feedback, committees, endless voice notes, «más WOW»,
«para ayer», urgencies that stop being urgent, meetings that could have been an
email, creatives vs accounts vs directors, and the agency's own contradictions.
**Never agency-vs-client warfare. Copywriters is a target too.**

## Episode test · CANON LOCK 8
> **ME RECONOZCO · ME SORPRENDE · ME ENCARIÑO — at least 2 of 3.**
A story that only gets a laugh nobody recognises, or that is recognisable but
never surprises, does not ship.

## Audience goal
Create affection and curiosity. Viewers should recognize characters, pick favorites, wonder what is behind the Server door and want another episode.
