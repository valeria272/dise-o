# REEL PRODUCTION PROTOCOL
1. Write 1-sentence premise.
2. Define hook visible/audible by second 0–2.
3. Beat sheet with timestamps.
4. Music arc before generating footage.
5. Shot list with lens, framing, action IN, action OUT.
6. For every adjacent shot specify transition logic.
7. Generate continuity keyframes in chains when shots must match.
8. Animate only after keyframes are approved.
9. Edit with sound design.
10. Review once MUTED (visual rhythm) and once AUDIO ONLY (music/SFX rhythm).
11. Reject dead air that is not an intentional comedy beat.
12. Final continuity check against WORLD_BIBLE.
