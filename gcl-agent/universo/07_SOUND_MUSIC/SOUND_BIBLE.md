# SOUND & MUSIC BIBLE

## G sonic identity
Original expressive robot micro-language: curious “eh?”, questioning chirp, pleased double chirp, skeptical descending tone, digital sigh, idea ping, soft startup. Friendly, premium, funny and lovable. Inspired by the emotional economy of cinematic robots, but never clone any existing character's exact voice or signature sounds.

## Character signatures
Marta: paper feed, rollers, mechanical clicks, occasional blunt beep.
R.01: wheels, soft motor, docking chime.
Server: deep fan/hum, relay clicks, low notification tone.
Portal: recurring clean DING.

## Music
No flat corporate bed. Each episode gets an arc. Territories: electro-funk, breakbeat, French house, indie electronic, garage, odd percussion, ironic orchestral/classical moments. Silence is a comedy tool.

Recurring sonic punctuation: portal DING + G micro-chirp + magenta visor boot.
